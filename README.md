# Out Of The Box!

Out Of The Box is a 3D puzzle-platformer starring Chip, a small robot toy who’s been left behind after being outgrown. Waking up in a room that feels both familiar and new, Chip must find a way out—and set off on a journey to freedom.

Chip isn’t an ordinary toy. They’re from the now-retired “Switchables” collection, a line of toys with interchangeable arm and leg modules, each with different abilities. Many modules are scattered around the room—find the ones you need to keep moving forward.

Each piece of furniture is its own little world. Explore the room and discover how Chip’s owner has changed over time through the spaces they’ve lived in.

Break free: explore your surroundings, solve puzzles, and find a way to open the door. It’s your only way out. 

This is a game being developed by students from <a href="https://www.imagecampus.edu.ar/">Image Campus</a>
